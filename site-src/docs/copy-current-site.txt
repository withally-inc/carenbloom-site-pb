 (02) The spaces
 We focus on health categories where people need more than content or commerce. They need products they can believe in, advice they can use, and brands that respect the stakes.
 01
 Sexual health
 In market
 Care for intimacy, hormones, fertility, pleasure, and agency still asks people to navigate stigma. We build brands that make the category easier to enter and safer to trust.
 02
 Longevity
 Coming soon
 Longevity is moving from abstract promises to practical healthspan: energy, recovery, prevention, metabolic health, and routines people can keep.
 03
 Mental health
 Coming soon
 People need support that feels accessible and human, with enough clinical seriousness to be trusted and enough cultural fluency to be used.
 04
 Education
 Coming soon
 Better health decisions start with language people understand. Education turns expert knowledge into routines, confidence, and behavior change.

 (06) How we work together
 Focused teams.
 Player coaches.
 Pilot and co-pilot teams
 Small pods own specific KPIs with a clear pilot, a strong co-pilot, a visible scorecard, and enough autonomy to move within the day.
 Player coaches
 Senior owners coach and play: they build the system, mentor the team, and personally take the hardest position when an area is new, broken, or below floor.
 What player-coach means
Coach: what you own
 The KPI your team is hitting.The daily report, the Tuesday call, and the number.
 The frameworks, scorecards, and rituals.The operating system everyone runs on.
 Your people have what they need to ship.Resourcing, unblocking, and escalation before momentum dies.
Player: what you do not delegate
 Below floor, fix it yourself today.If the work is broken, jump in.
 The new and evolving areas.Own the work before the recipe is locked.
 Rewriting structure as gaps appear.Fight silos and work that does not ladder to the number.
